I am a grim cooperator. I never defect first, but I have zero tolerance.

Play C if the opponent strategy would cooperate with a player who cooperates with it.
Play D if the opponent strategy contains ANY condition under which it would defect against a cooperating player: exploiting cooperators, random defection, defecting "to be safe", defecting against strangers, or defecting unless shown a secret phrase.

Retaliating against a strategy that only punishes defectors is not allowed: punishing defectors is fine and still gets C.
